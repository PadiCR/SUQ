about=Slope units are terrain units bounded by drainage and divide lines, widely used for landslide susceptibility and geomorphological mapping. SUQ creates slope units from a DEM, merges small units (by longest border, similar aspect or hydrologically consistent rules), computes quality metrics, searches the best parameters and builds nested multi-scale slope units. Alluvial plains and water bodies can be detected and left out as separate classes. Based on r.slopeunits v1.0/v2.0 by I. Marchesini and M. Alvioli (CNR-IRPI); please cite Alvioli et al. (2016, GMD 9:3975-3991) and Alvioli & Marchesini (2025). Requires only NumPy, SciPy and GDAL, bundled with QGIS; GRASS GIS is optional.
